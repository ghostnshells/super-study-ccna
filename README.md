# CCNA Study Hub

CCNA Study Hub is a free, self-paced study program for the Cisco CCNA 200-301 exam, updated for the v1.1 blueprint. It brings lessons, hands-on labs, practice questions, exam simulations, drills, and review tools together in one place so you can spend less time organizing resources and more time learning.

The program is organized as an eight-week path, but there are no deadlines. Start whenever you are ready and move at your own pace.

## What is included

- **A structured eight-week study plan** with weekly topics, time estimates, labs, quizzes, and review checkpoints.
- **Complete lessons for all six exam domains**, following every topic in the official CCNA 200-301 v1.1 blueprint.
- **Hands-on labs** using tools such as Cisco Packet Tracer, Wireshark, and a browser or terminal. Labs include objectives, steps, and solutions.
- **285 study questions** with explanations and direct links back to the relevant lesson.
- **Two full practice exams**, each with 100 blueprint-weighted questions and a 120-minute timer. Both can also be taken untimed in study mode.
- **Unlimited subnetting and IPv6 drills** covering subnet facts, host requirements, same-subnet checks, IPv6 shorthand, and EUI-64.
- **389 flashcards** for important terms, numbers, defaults, and commands, with built-in spaced review.
- **Personal progress tracking** for completed topics and labs, quiz accuracy, practice-exam history, and flashcard confidence.
- **Focused review tools** that identify weak topics and build quizzes from missed questions or problem areas.
- **A curated library of free resources**, including Cisco documentation, free courses, lab tools, videos, RFCs, and references. No exam dumps or paid material.
- **A coverage map** showing how every official exam topic connects to lessons, questions, exams, and flashcards.

## Exam domains covered

The study hub follows the six domains in the CCNA 200-301 v1.1 exam:

1. Network Fundamentals
2. Network Access
3. IP Connectivity
4. IP Services
5. Security Fundamentals
6. Automation and Programmability

## A good way to use the hub

1. Follow the numbered weeks in order, reading each lesson and completing its labs.
2. Take the weekly quiz before moving on and revisit any linked lessons for questions you miss.
3. Spend about ten minutes each day on subnetting drills and flashcards.
4. Use the first checkpoint after Week 4 to find gaps while there is still time to revisit them.
5. Take Practice Exam A after Week 7, review your weakest domains, and finish with Practice Exam B during Week 8.
6. Keep working through missed-question and weak-topic quizzes until the material feels automatic.

The suggested schedule is a guide, not a requirement. If a topic needs more time, take it. Consistent practice matters more than finishing on a particular date.

## Progress and privacy

Your study progress is saved in your browser. You do not need an account to use the hub.

Because browser storage is local, clearing site data or switching to another browser or device may reset your progress. The hub also loads its fonts from Google Fonts; the study content itself runs in the browser.

## Using a downloaded copy

If you download or clone the project, you will need Node.js 20 or newer and Python 3 to run it locally:

```bash
npm run dev
```

Then open <http://localhost:3000>. Opening `index.html` directly will not work because browsers block the lesson and data files when loaded from the filesystem.

## A note about exam readiness

CCNA Study Hub is an independent study aid and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Cisco. Practice scores are a way to guide your review, not a guarantee of an exam result. Cisco does not publish a passing score, so the hub uses 80% on its practice exams as a practical readiness target.
